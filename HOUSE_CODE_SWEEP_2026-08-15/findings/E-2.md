- direction: Existing seam: the repo's source-scan gate idiom (footgun #4 in CLAUDE.md, `bodyOf()` in `step_value_set_gate`) can pin the tail's call ORDER cheaply — one gate asserting `indexOf` monotonicity for the ~10 load-bearing pairs (sends-before-movers is *not* assertable as correct, but reconcile-after-movers, SET-before-suppress, addedTransport-after-relocate, heat-before-serviceDaySends are).
